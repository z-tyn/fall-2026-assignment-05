Why Artisan Roast Co.

The Art & Science of Superior Coffee

Every bean tells a story of soil, elevation, and harvest. We honor that journey through meticulous roasting profiles and thoughtful curation.

Ethical Direct Trade

We forge long-term partnerships directly with smallholder coffee cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, paying well above fair-trade minimums.

Micro-Batch Roasting

Each batch is roasted on our custom cast-iron drum roasters to bring out each micro-lot's innate natural sweetness, acidity, and delicate aromatics.

Peak Freshness Guarantee

All whole-bean coffees are packaged in degassing-valve airtight pouches within 24 hours of roasting and shipped directly to your door.
