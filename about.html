Our Roots & Heritage

Born from a Passion for Coffee Craftsmanship

Founded in 2018 in Portland, Oregon, Artisan Roast Co. began as an ambitious garage project between two certified Q-Graders who believed commercial coffee was leaving behind the true soul of the coffee bean.

Over the past eight years, we have grown into a dedicated roastery and tasting room. We roast on custom-modified vintage cast-iron drum roasters, relying on sensory precision, thermodynamic control, and cupping scores above 86 points for every lot we bring in.

Our Mission

“To honor every hand that touches the coffee cherry—from altitude farmer to meticulous roaster—by roasting with uncompromising precision, practicing radical direct trade, and empowering home brewers with exceptional tools and knowledge.”

Our Commitments

How We Roast & Operate

Direct-Trade Transparency

We negotiate directly with farm cooperatives. By cutting out intermediary brokers, up to 300% more revenue goes back into living wages, agronomic training, and healthcare for farming communities.

Data-Driven Profiling

Every roast curve is recorded with real-time rate-of-rise (RoR) monitoring and optical density spectrophotometry, ensuring identical cup excellence across every release.

Zero-Waste Packaging

From 100% commercially compostable bean bags to recycled unbleached shipping cartons, sustainability informs every packaging and shipping decision.
